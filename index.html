About

I am a Technical Trainer, Instructional and Learning Experience Designer with over 15 years of experience designing and delivering impactful learning for adult learners from diverse professional, cultural, and educational backgrounds. My work focuses on transforming complex technical concepts into clear, structured, and outcome-driven learning experiences.
